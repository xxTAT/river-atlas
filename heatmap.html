INTERACTION MAPS

See the evidence behind
each structure

RIVER localizes duplex-associated structural features in two-dimensional interaction maps and infers structures from them. Choose a dataset to pan and zoom its normalized map, overlay the structural features, and mark any structure's base pairs on the map to check its experimental support.

STEP 1

Choose a dataset

Each row is one virus–sample–method combination. Click a row to load its map. Maps are normalized with Juicer tools (KR balancing or observed/expected).
