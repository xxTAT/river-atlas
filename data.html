DATA & CITATION

Data and citation

All structures, attribute tables and interaction maps in RIVER Atlas can be downloaded without registration. This page lists where the data come from, how the files are organized and how to cite them.

Data DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21543755 · License: CC BY 4.0 · No new sequencing data were generated in this study

Download

Complete per-dataset bundles are archived on Zenodo. Single structures and filtered attribute tables can also be exported directly from the Results page.

File formats

ContentFormatNotes
Secondary structures.dbn · .ct · .bedpeDot-bracket and connectivity table; readable by VARNA, RNAstructure, ViennaRNA, forna and similar tools.
Structure and base-pair attributes.jsonFields include coordinates, span, range class, interaction support, pairing ambiguity, conservation and category labels.
Structural features.bed · .bedpeCoordinates of the two arms of each duplex-associated feature.
Interaction maps.hic · .matrixMatrices used for structural analysis and reconstruction.

How to cite

THE RIVER PAPER

Deng Y., Liu Z., Chen L., . RIVER: duplex-morphology-guided inference of RNA secondary structures and their alternatives from RNA–RNA interaction maps.

When citing the data themselves, please also give the Zenodo DOI.

doi:10.5281/zenodo.21543755

In addition, please cite the studies that generated the original interaction data (see the dataset table).

Before you use the data

  • Structures are computational inferences from ensemble-averaged interaction data. Well-folded and alternative structures are candidates to guide hypotheses and experimental validation, not experimentally confirmed conformations.
  • Under the GISAID terms of use, the atlas provides only the derived SARS-CoV-2 conservation scores and does not redistribute GISAID sequences.
  • Data are released under CC BY 4.0; the website source code is released under the MIT License.
